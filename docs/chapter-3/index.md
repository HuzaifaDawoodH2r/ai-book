# Chapter 3: Decision Making in Physical AI

This chapter examines how Physical AI systems process information from multiple sensors and make decisions that result in physical actions. You'll explore algorithms for planning, control, and decision-making in dynamic environments.

## Lessons in this Chapter

- [Lesson 3.1: Planning and Pathfinding in Physical AI](./lesson-3-1.md)
- [Lesson 3.2: Control Systems and Actuator Management](./lesson-3-2.md)
- [Lesson 3.3: Uncertainty and Decision Making](./lesson-3-3.md)

## Overview

Decision making is the process by which Physical AI systems analyze sensory data and determine appropriate actions. In this chapter, you will explore:

- Planning algorithms for navigation and task execution
- Control systems that translate decisions into physical actions
- Methods for handling uncertainty in sensor data and environments
- Reinforcement learning approaches for decision making
- Safety considerations in autonomous systems

Effective decision-making systems are crucial for Physical AI applications that must operate reliably in complex, dynamic environments.
