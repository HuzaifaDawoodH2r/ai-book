# Chapter 2: Perception in Physical AI

This chapter explores how Physical AI systems perceive and understand their environment through various sensing modalities, from basic sensors to sophisticated computer vision and audio processing systems.

## Lessons in this Chapter

- [Lesson 2.1: Computer Vision in Physical AI](./lesson-2-1.md)
- [Lesson 2.2: Audio and Speech Processing](./lesson-2-2.md)
- [Lesson 2.3: Multi-Modal Perception Systems](./lesson-2-3.md)

## Overview

Perception is a critical component of Physical AI, enabling systems to understand and interpret their environment. In this chapter, you will explore:

- Computer vision techniques for visual perception
- Audio processing for auditory understanding
- Multi-modal systems that combine different sensing modalities
- Sensor fusion techniques for comprehensive environment understanding
- Challenges in real-world perception systems

Understanding perception systems is crucial for developing effective Physical AI applications that can operate reliably in diverse real-world environments.
